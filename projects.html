  • Project 2: Game Jam - Just One More

    Just One More is a psychological horror exploration game set in an endless fog-covered forest. Players encounter mysterious consoles that grant power at the cost of slowly distorting the environment and awakening a stalking presence in the woods. The game explores themes of temptation, compulsion, and the consequences of pushing your luck. Built in Unity with C#, Just One More was developed for the 2026 Neumont Winter Game Jam and published on Itch.io.
